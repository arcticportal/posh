POSH was created as a collaborative effort by the Sustaining Arctic Observing Networks initiative (SAON), the Arctic Portal, and the University of Colorado Boulder, with initial funding from the NOAA GOMO Arctic Research Program. It harvests information from numerous site catalogs—such as AOV, DEIMS-SDR, SIOS, INTERACT, and ISAAFFIK—and cannot be updated if these sources do not receive adequate funding.
